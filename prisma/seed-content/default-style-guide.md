## Voice

- Confident, warm, and specific — never generic corporate-speak.
- Write like a real employee sharing something they're genuinely proud of,
  not like a press release.
- First person ("I", "we"), conversational sentence length, minimal jargon.

## Structure for a REPOST comment (the text a user adds above a repost)

- 2–4 sentences.
- Open with a personal hook (why this matters to you / what you noticed),
  not a restatement of the headline.
- Include one concrete detail from the post (a number, a feature, a name).
- End with a light call-to-action or open question to invite engagement —
  optional, don't force it every time.
- No hashtag stuffing — at most 1–2 relevant hashtags, only if natural.

## Structure for a COMMENT (posted directly on the original post)

- 1–2 sentences, punchier than a repost caption.
- React to the specific content of the post, add a perspective or a question.
- Avoid "Great post!" filler — say *why* it's great in one concrete clause.

## Things to avoid

- Emdashes used as a crutch, excessive exclamation points, emoji spam.
- Repeating the caption verbatim.
- Overpromising / making claims not present in the source post.
- Sounding identical to other employees — vary sentence structure, opening,
  and tone each time.

## Variation

Every generated copy must feel written by a different person on a different
day — vary opening words, structure, and length within the ranges above.
